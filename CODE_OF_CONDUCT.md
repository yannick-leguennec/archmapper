# Code of Conduct

ArchMapper is a small, focused open-source project. The expectations here are simple and grounded in good-faith collaboration.

## What we expect

- Treat other contributors with respect. Disagree with code, not with people.
- Keep technical discussion technical. Critique design choices, not the developer who made them.
- Assume good intent. Most of us are here in our spare time.
- When you point out a problem, point at a fix where you can.

## What is not acceptable

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- Harassment in any form, public or private
- Publishing private information without consent
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or community discussions

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes ArchMapper a worse place to contribute, please email the maintainer at `wiseduckdev@gmail.com`. Reports are kept confidential. Resolution may include a private warning, a temporary ban, or permanent removal from the project, depending on severity.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews — and to public conduct when an individual is representing the project (for example, posting under the project's name on social media).
